About Me

Builder first. Full-stack by practice.

I am Harikaran Chettiyar, but most people call me Hari. I am a Computer Science graduate based in Pune who enjoys taking unclear requirements and turning them into working, deployed software — from the interface and backend to domains, hosting, and handover.

My work spans web platforms, mobile application development, Linux automation, and Android systems. Moving between these areas has made me a faster learner and a patient debugger, and taught me to focus on the problem, the people using the product, and the most practical way to deliver it.
