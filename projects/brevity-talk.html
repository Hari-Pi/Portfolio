About the project

Brevity Talk lets a fan book a personal live video moment, about 20 seconds long, with an actor, athlete or comedian, for a birthday, anniversary or another occasion. The fan keeps the recording.

Creators set their own price and are paid for the time they are live, and brands can sponsor moments.

My Role

I built the whole product as its Full-stack Developer, including its public website.

What I Delivered

  • The whole product: not only the website, but the platform itself.
  • The public website: a home page that explains how it works, pages for creators and for brands, a FAQ, a safety page, and the privacy, refund and terms pages.

Outcome

The product is live at brevitytalks.com, and its public site explains how it works to fans, creators and brands.
