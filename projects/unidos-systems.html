About the project

Unidos Systems is a data analytics and AI governance company based in Pune. It helps teams in many industries, such as banking, insurance, healthcare and logistics, see what their data says, keep it trustworthy and use AI responsibly.

Its website explains the three services in plain words and makes it easy to get in touch.

My Role

I designed and built the website, wrote and edited the text across the pages, set up the domain, hosting and search basics, and I keep the site up to date.

What I Delivered

  • A clear home page: what the company does, who it helps and how to start a conversation.
  • One page for each service: data analytics, data governance and AI governance, each explained on its own page.
  • Clear text: I wrote or edited the copy across all pages so it reads simply.
  • Domain, hosting and search setup: the site runs on its own domain and has the basics in place so people can find it.

Outcome

The company has a clear website on its own domain that explains its three services in separate, easy pages, and it is kept up to date.

Tech Stack

  • Front end: Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no framework and no build step
