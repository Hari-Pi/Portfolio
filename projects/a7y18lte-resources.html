About the project

a7y18lte-Resources is a dedicated hub for customizing the Samsung Galaxy A7 2018 (SM-A750x). It provides advanced users with custom kernels, de-googled stock ROMs, TWRP/PBRP recovery images, and extensive guides for flashing GSIs (Generic System Images) to breathe new life into the device.

Key Features

  • Custom Boot Kernels for OneUI and GSIs
  • De-googled Android 10 Stock ROM
  • TWRP and PBRP custom recovery builds
  • Detailed TrebleDroid, /e/ OS, and LineageOS GSI guides
  • Magisk root upgrade methodologies
