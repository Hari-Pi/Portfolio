About the project

Carrier Wave Technology teaches PLC, HMI, VFD and SCADA programming and builds electrical control panels, from Pirangut near Pune.

The company gives certificates to its learners, so it needed a website to present its training and services, and a simple way for anyone to check that a certificate is real.

My Role

I built both the company website and the certificate system, and set up the hosting and domain.

What I Delivered

  • Company website: training courses, services, gallery, reviews and contact details in one place.
  • Certificate check page: anyone can enter a roll number or certificate number and see whether it is real.
  • Staff page for issuing certificates: a sign-in page where staff create certificates.
  • Hosting on Cloudflare: one Cloudflare Worker runs the website and the certificate tools, and each update goes live automatically.

Outcome

The company has one website for its training and services, and its certificates can be checked online by anyone, at any time.

Tech Stack

  • Front end: HTML, CSS and JavaScript
  • Back end and hosting: Cloudflare Workers with a custom domain
